2. DEVICES
iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26.6 (physical). Deployment target iOS 17.6.

3. FUNCTION / AUDIENCE
An English vocabulary tool for Chinese-speaking learners. The user photographs a page; the app recognizes every English word in it, attaches IPA, part of speech, Chinese definition, etymology and an example sentence, then schedules review with SM-2 spaced repetition. Words can also be reviewed by listening, screen locked.
Audience: students preparing for CET-4/6, IELTS or TOEFL; readers of English books; commuters. No ads.

4. ACCESS

appreview@deepalpha.club / AppReview2026
The sign-in screen has a phone/email toggle at the top. Phone codes come from a mainland-China SMS provider and cannot reach non-Chinese numbers, so please tap "email" (0:48). The account is pre-populated with words in all review states and two playlists.
Photo recognition takes 15-50s; if camera access is denied, "choose from album" still works, no permission needed. Dictation accepts typing or the microphone.
Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio): Settings > Pronunciation > enable "auto play" (off by default), open the review tab, press play, lock the screen - playback continues and the Lock Screen shows the current word (3:08). No other background activity.
Account deletion: Settings > Delete account, re-enter password; immediate and permanent (4:04).

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Google Gemini: recognizes the words and generates the definitions. The image is processed in real time, never written to disk or to our database, and carries no user identity.
- Apple SFSpeechRecognizer: dictation grading; audio is handled by iOS, never reaching our servers.
- MiniMax text-to-speech: pronunciation audio, proxied through our backend.
- Alibaba Cloud: email and SMS codes. At our tier the carrier allows no custom SMS signature, so the code arrives under the provider's generic one (0:32); the registration screen warns users.
- Railway (hosting/DB), Cloudflare (DNS).
No advertising or analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking, so no ATT prompt.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Features and content are identical everywhere. Only account creation differs: email works worldwide, while phone registration uses a mainland-China SMS provider (see section 4).

7. PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry; no protected third-party material. Definitions, IPA, etymologies and examples are generated by Gemini at recognition time, not copied from any dictionary. All pronunciation audio is synthesized by MiniMax, a commercial service we subscribe to.

8. IN-APP SUBSCRIPTION (new in this build)
Every feature is free; only daily volume is capped - 3 photo recognitions and 20 new words per day, with unlimited review of words already studied. WordLens Pro Monthly (club.deepalpha.wordlens.pro.monthly, CNY 29/month, auto-renewable) lifts both caps. The paywall opens when either cap is reached, and any time from Settings > Upgrade to Pro. It states the price, the term, the auto-renewal disclosure and a Restore Purchases button, and links to our Terms and Privacy Policy. It funds recurring per-call costs: vision recognition and speech synthesis. The demo account is free, so the flow is reproducible.
