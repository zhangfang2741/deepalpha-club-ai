2. DEVICES
iPhone 16 Pro, iOS 26.6 (physical device). Deployment target iOS 17.6.

3. FUNCTION / AUDIENCE
An English vocabulary tool for Chinese-speaking learners. Looking words up while reading and copying them into a notebook is slow. The user photographs a page; the app recognizes every English word in it, attaches IPA, part of speech, Chinese definition, etymology and an example sentence, and schedules review with SM-2 spaced repetition. Words can also be reviewed by listening, even while the screen is locked.
Audience: students preparing for CET-4/6, postgraduate exams, IELTS or TOEFL; readers of English books; commuters. Free, no ads, no IAP.

The sign-in screen has a phone/email toggle at the top. Phone codes come from a mainland-China SMS provider and cannot reach non-Chinese numbers, so please tap "email" (see 0:48). The account is pre-populated with words in all review states and two playlists.
Tab 1 photo capture (recognition takes 15-50s; if camera access is denied, "choose from album" still works and needs no permission); tab 2 review and dictation (typing needs no permission, microphone optional); tab 3 vocabulary; tab 4 settings.
Background audio (UIBackgroundModes: audio): Settings > Pronunciation > enable "auto play" (off by default so users are not surprised by sound), return to tab 2, press play, lock the screen - playback continues and the Lock Screen shows the current word (3:08). No other background activity.
Account deletion: Settings > Delete account, re-enter password; immediate and permanent (4:04).

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Google Gemini: recognizes words in the photo and generates the definitions. The image is sent for real-time processing only, never stored on disk or in our database, and carries no user identity.
- Apple SFSpeechRecognizer: dictation grading; audio is handled by iOS, never reaching our servers.
- MiniMax text-to-speech: pronunciation audio, proxied through our backend so the provider key never ships in the app.
- Alibaba Cloud DirectMail / Number Verification: delivers email and SMS codes. Carrier rules bar a custom SMS signature at our tier, so the message carries the provider's generic signature rather than the app name (see 0:32); the registration screen tells users to expect this, so it is not mistaken for spam.
- Railway (hosting/database), Cloudflare (DNS).
No advertising or analytics SDKs and no cross-app tracking, so no ATT prompt.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
Features and content are identical everywhere. Only account creation differs: email registration works worldwide, while phone registration relies on a mainland-China SMS provider, so non-Chinese numbers cannot receive the code and should use email. Both create the same account type.

7. PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry; no protected third-party material. Definitions, IPA, etymologies and example sentences are generated by Gemini at recognition time, not copied from any dictionary. All pronunciation audio is synthesized by MiniMax, a commercial service we subscribe to. No licensed dictionary content or recordings are bundled.
