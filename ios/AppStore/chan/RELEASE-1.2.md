```
1. DEMO ACCOUNT
Email: appreview@deepalpha.club / Password: AppReview2026
Use the "Email" tab. Lessons are also viewable without an account ("Browse the Chan primer").

2. WHAT'S NEW IN 1.2 AND WHERE TO FIND IT
- Analysis tab (opens by default): AAPL is pre-filled; tap Analyze. The result shows a summary card, the chart with Chan structures and a MACD subchart. Tap any buy/sell label for "how it was detected". Below the chart are the "Fundamentals" and "Analyst ratings" sections.
- Radar tab: top card = broad-market regime and sector strength; the bubble field shows recent Chan buy/sell-type structures among index stocks whose composite fundamentals grade meets a threshold. Non-subscribers can open one sample day (marked "Sample").
- Fundamentals section: five within-industry dimensions shown as letter grades. The "Latest report" card opens the company's latest periodic / annual report inside the app. The round button at the bottom right of the reader opens an AI-generated summary of key points.
- Profile -> Preferences -> Signal rules: strict / medium / loose.
- Profile -> Contact us: feedback form (email + message), sent to us by email; declared as Customer Support data.

3. NATURE OF CONTENT
This is a technical-analysis and education app about Chan theory. It is not an investment advisory service and does not recommend securities, issue trade instructions or promise returns. "Buy 1/2/3" and "Sell 1/2/3" are Chan theory's standard names for chart-structure positions, not trade recommendations. The radar does not score or rank stocks; the fundamentals threshold only narrows which stocks are shown. Letter grades describe reported financial data relative to industry peers and make no forecast. Disclaimers are shown on the sign-in screen, the Radar screen, the analysis result screen, the Fundamentals section and in Profile -> About.

4. AI-GENERATED CONTENT
The report summary is generated by a large language model only from the text of the public report. It is labeled "AI summary", states that it may contain errors and that the original report prevails, and is filtered to exclude buy/sell wording. Only public report text is sent to the model; no personal data.

5. THIRD-PARTY DOCUMENTS
Company reports are public regulatory filings (SEC EDGAR for US; exchange announcements for China A-share and Hong Kong). A-share analyst rating rows can open the publicly posted research report for reading; these broker reports can be read in the app but cannot be saved or shared from it. US/HK rating rows link to the related public news article, opened in an in-app viewer restricted to that website (other links open in Safari).

6. SUBSCRIPTION
One auto-renewing monthly subscription, club.deepalpha.chan.pro.monthly ("DeepAlpha Membership", 128 CNY/month in China). Eligible first-time subscribers get an introductory price of 58 CNY for the first month; there is no free trial. It includes unlimited analysis (free: 3 distinct tickers per day), the sub-level view, the daily radar with 30 trading days of history, the fundamentals list, and an unlimited watchlist. Existing subscribers of this same product keep their current price. Paywall: "Subscribe" in the quota bar on the Analysis tab, or Profile -> Subscription -> View Plans. It shows the price, the intro offer with the renewal price, the auto-renewal disclosure, Terms and Privacy links and Restore Purchases. A sandbox account can purchase and restore.

7. PERMISSIONS AND DATA
- Photos: write-only (add-only), requested only when the user taps "Save to Photos" in the share preview.
- This version does not request notification permission.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or ad tracking (SKAdNetwork only, no IDFA).

8. ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile -> Account & Security -> Delete account (with confirmation); permanently deletes the account and its data.

Network access is required; market data comes from public market-data APIs.
```

## 仍有的风险（需要你判断，代码没改）

- **中国大陆的生成式 AI 规定**：App 在中国区上架、1.2 新增了「AI 总结」。Apple 近年对中国区带生成式 AI 功能的 App 会要求提供生成式 AI 服务备案。
  这是基于公开财报的要点整理、不是对话机器人，风险相对低，但如果被问到，可选做法：①补备案；②中国区隐藏 AI 总结按钮（我可以改）。
- **券商研报**：仍可在 App 内阅读（会员或免费均可）。若审核以 5.2.2 质疑，回复「这些是券商在公开平台发布的研报，App 只提供阅读、不提供下载」；还不行就改为跳转 Safari 打开。
- **登录墙（5.1.1(v)）**：除教程外，分析功能需要登录。1.0 / 1.1 都以此通过，但审核员换人时可能被提出。
